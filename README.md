**The World in One Picture — Thursday 19 November 2026.** A newspaper front page that sums up the whole day in a single 1024×1024 image: a masthead naming the day in full, then the day's six biggest stories, each with a hand-drawn flat illustration, a short headline and a one-line factual caption. Every story happened — or had its decisive moment — on Thursday 19 November 2026, and each was cross-checked against at least two news sources on the day.

The six stories on the front page:

1. **Trump, Xi, Putin Meet in Shenzhen** (diplomacy) — the three presidents hold their first-ever trilateral meeting at Kylin Villa on the sidelines of the APEC summit, with nuclear arms control topping the agenda.
2. **Iran Says Tankers Ablaze in Hormuz** (conflict) — Tehran says three oil tankers struck naval mines and caught fire east of the Strait of Hormuz, as the Gulf war spreads to shipping.
3. **World Court Rules on Rohingya Case** (law) — verdict day at The Hague in the genocide case over Myanmar's treatment of the Rohingya.
4. **Anthropic Makes Its Nasdaq Debut** (markets) — the Claude maker starts trading under the ticker ANT, priced at $78 a share and valued at about $1.4 trillion.
5. **GTA VI Launches Around the World** (culture) — Grand Theft Auto VI goes on sale worldwide to near-perfect reviews, and gamers return to Vice City after a 13-year wait.
6. **Van Gogh Fetches Record $190M** (arts) — a Van Gogh painting sells for a record $190.4 million after a dispute over its Nazi-era history.

How it works: the finished poster is committed as `poster.png`, and `main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) verifies the PNG and copies it byte-for-byte to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (falling back to `/output/entry.png`). Two runs always produce identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
